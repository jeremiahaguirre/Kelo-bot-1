    Every tree he chopped, everything he planted, his roads, hills, holes, dirt piles, new land and builds, on a map of the island. Tap a mark or a row to fly there.

    How to explore

    Two ways to watch: Follow keeps Kelo-1 in view wherever he goes. Explore lets you fly anywhere yourself. Moving the map switches to Explore; Where is Kelo-1? brings you back.

    Mouse

    • Drag the ground to slide the map
    • Right-drag to turn and tilt
    • Scroll to zoom toward the cursor
    • Double-click a spot to fly there
    • Click the ground: Fly here, Send Kelo-1 here, or What's this?
    • Click the Storehouse or Museum to look inside

    Keys

    • W A S D or the arrow keys to move
    • Q and E to turn
    • R and F to tilt
    • + and − to zoom
    • K to fly back to Kelo-1
    • ? opens this card

    Touch

    • One finger slides the map
    • Pinch to zoom, twist two fingers to turn
    • The joystick moves you around
    • Tap the ground for the spot menu

    Map and places

    • The map shows Kelo-1 (arrow), his vehicles, digs and builds, and what you can see
    • Click the map to fly there
    • Places jumps to any building, dig or project
